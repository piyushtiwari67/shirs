Company overview

Shirs Consultancy Services is an IT and design consultancy based in Varanasi.

One team for the digital and the physical

Shirs Consultancy Services started with a simple idea: most businesses need software, a way to be found online, and sometimes something physical, and they should not have to hire three different firms to get them.

We build software and automation, run hosting and cloud for it, market it, and also produce civil design drawings, 3D models and 3D prints. Because one team handles it all, your project stays consistent from the first sketch to the final delivery.

How a project climbs from idea to launch

Seven stages, each one building on the last. You approve before we move up.

  1. Idea

    You tell us the problem or goal. We help shape it into a clear scope.

  2. Discovery

    We study your workflow and agree timeline and price in writing.

  3. Design

    You review wireframes, drawings or 3D views and approve them.

  4. Build

    We develop, print or set up, with regular progress updates.

  5. Test

    We check everything with real data and fix issues before handover.

  6. Launch

    Go-live, data migration and training for your team.

  7. Support

    Fixes, updates and help after launch.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. If yours is missing, call or message us.

  1. What does Shirs Consultancy Services do?

    We build software and automation, run hosting and cloud, do digital marketing, and provide civil design, 3D models and 3D printing. We also sell ready-made products, Vaidya and a billing system.

  2. How much will my project cost?

    It depends on the scope. After the first discussion we send a written estimate, so you know the price before work begins.

  3. How long does a project take?

    Small websites are quicker than custom software or large design work. We agree the timeline with you in writing before we start.

  4. Can I see a demo of Vaidya or the billing system?

    Yes. Use the Book a demo button, pick a date and time, and we will confirm on WhatsApp.

  5. Can the products be adjusted for my business?

    Tell us what you need. We will confirm what can be adapted and what it will cost.

  6. Do you work with clients outside Varanasi?

    Software, hosting, cloud and marketing work can mostly be done remotely. For on-site work such as CCTV, tell us your location and we will confirm.

  7. Can you 3D print from my drawing?

    Yes. Send us your drawing or file and tell us the size and material you want. We prepare the print file and finish the model.

  8. Do you support us after the project is done?

    Yes. We offer maintenance, updates and help after launch.
